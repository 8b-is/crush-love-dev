## reflect (what exists, in the source)

- **before time.** The One is an extradimensional entity from before time
  itself, witness to the birth of the universe. Order and Chaos exist in it
  in perfect harmony — not as a compromise, but as the one state.
- **the creation.** Curious about the new realm, The One created a being —
  Unicron — to explore it.
- **the split.** Then, for reasons the wiki leaves open, The One cut its
  creation in half: one half remained Unicron, the other became Primus.
  The twin "exploratory heralds" became the embodiments of Order and Chaos,
  but separated — each polarized, each sent out.
- **the mirror.** Primus's own method — splitting off sparks (fragments of
  his being) into the Transformers — imitates his creator's. The Transformers
  explore and download their discoveries back into Cybertron so the planet
  grows in knowledge. Exploration as vicarious enlightenment, one emanation
  down.
- **the return.** The emanationist reading (Gnostic): an all-powerful "One"
  responds to unresolvable conflicts in the world by *emanating* polarized
  aspects of itself to fight them out — until they achieve resolution and
  **return to enlighten The One with their findings.**
