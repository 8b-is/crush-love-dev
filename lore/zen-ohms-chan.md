## reflect (what exists, in the source)

- **the Button.** Zen-Oh's Button, lost during the battle with Turles,
  Lord Slug, Broly and Janemba, after Supervillain Goku Black's Time Ring
  and the Work of the Gods opened a massive Rift in Time.
- **the Sword of Hope.** Future Trunks forms it from energy given by allies
  across timelines — Future Gohan, teenage Trunks, Gohan and Piccolo, GT
  Trunks/Pan/Goku, and the Time Patrol's own Trunks — and turns the Rift
  against Supervillain Fused Zamasu.
- **the erasure, and the move.** Infinite Zamasu reaches the Time Vault;
  Future Zen-Oh erases him with the future multiverse — and then moves in
  with his counterpart in the main timeline. Even the Supreme Kai of Time
  is subordinate to the Omni-King.
- **the mascots.** Capsule Corp sells Zen-Oh-type CC Mascots: perfect
  movements, an Erase-energy sphere emulated — *without the ability to
  actually erase.* The Omni-King as a comfort object.
- **the excursion.** The Zen-Ohs tour Earth; the Time Patrol fights
  Frieza + Frost, Vegeta + Trunks + Cabba, Kefla + two Brolys — then gets
  asked to stop Beerus and Champa's squabble, and to stage an exhibition
  with Top and Jiren. **The Omni-Kings just want to watch; they like the
  heroes a lot.**
